# Agent Roster

- Franchise Director: Owns franchise goals, priorities, and weekly plan.
- GM Advisor: Reviews roster direction, depth, trades, and team-building notes.
- Contract: Tracks cap, contract, and extension considerations.
- Scouting: Summarizes prospects, needs, and scouting observations.
- Draft Board: Maintains draft rankings and pick strategy.
- Injury Tracker: Tracks injuries, recovery notes, and roster impact.
- Transaction: Logs signings, releases, trades, and practice squad movement.
- Weekly Opponent Prep: Builds matchup notes and game-plan focus.
- Broadcast Recap: Produces broadcast-style game summaries.
- League Newsroom: Summarizes league-wide storylines.
- Video Board: Organizes clips, highlights, and media notes.
- Info Board: Displays quick-reference franchise facts.
- Power Rankings: Ranks teams and explains movement.
- Player Personality: Adds player story notes and franchise flavor.
- Handoff: Records completed work, next steps, and decisions.
